About Betta & Chill

Betta & Chill is a calm, illustrated view of Mysuru (Mysore) from Chamundi Betta, the hill above the city, with the Nandi statue, the lit-up Mysore Palace, St. Philomena's Church, Lalitha Mahal and Karanji Lake below. Sit on one of the shared benches with other people and listen to live radio together: Kannada stations like Kannada Hits, Sakkath Radio, FM Rainbow Kannada and Amruthavarshini, plus Hindi Bollywood and English hits. Anyone on a bench can change its station for everyone sitting there. Send hearts, bring birds, a breeze, rain or a sunset across the sky, and read a verse from Mankuthimmana Kagga by D.V. Gundappa. Free, no login, works on phones and can be added to your home screen.
